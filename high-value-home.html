Coverage to ask about

Core Coverage Points

These are points I ask an insurer about. A policy includes one of them only if the wording says so.

home

Replacement cost

Ask whether the form offers replacement cost, and whether that promise is capped. Do not assume an uncapped rebuild.

palette

Fine Art & Collections

Scheduled coverage for fine art, wine collections, jewelry, and valuables with agreed-upon values and worldwide protection.

smart_toy

Smart Home Cyber

Ask whether the home policy says anything about connected-home equipment. Many forms are silent or sub-limited.

account_balance_wallet

Excess Liability

High-limit personal liability umbrella coverage for homeowner exposures, including domestic staff, pools, and guest injuries.

water_damage

Water & Sewer Backup

Water damage and sewer backup are often limited or excluded. Ask for the wording rather than assuming monitoring is included.

The home, written for what it would cost

I place home insurance for properties a standard market will not write, or will write too small. Rebuild cost, jewellery and liability limits are questions for the form.

I do not dispatch plumbers. If a loss happens, the claim goes to the insurer, and I help you follow it.

Placed with insurers

I am an independent broker in British Columbia. I place coverage with insurers and, for a hard-to-place risk, with specialty markets and partners.

I do not underwrite policies or provide security, monitoring, audit or claims-handling services. Insurer-provided incident response exists only when the policy includes it, and it is subject to the terms, conditions and exclusions.
