What this site collects

If you use the contact form or the booking calendar, you send information such as your name, email, phone number, company and whatever you type about the insurance question. Those tools are hosted by LeadConnector (HighLevel). The site also loads a chat widget from the same provider. If you use chat, the messages you type are collected by that service so I can reply.

The site uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-RT7FQNEV2G) to see aggregated visits. That can include a cookie, your browser, and the pages you opened. There is no mailing list. Keep policy numbers, health details and passwords out of a chat box.

Why

This information is used to answer you, book a coverage review, prepare a submission to an insurer, and keep the records a broker is required to keep. Personal information is never sold.

Who else sees it

A quote may mean sending relevant details to insurers and, for a hard-to-place risk, to wholesale partners. LeadConnector processes the form, booking and chat. Google processes analytics. This site does not ask for a street address.

How long

Inquiry records are kept long enough to handle the request and to meet insurance record-keeping rules in British Columbia. Analytics data is kept on Google's schedule for that property.

Your rights

Federal privacy law (PIPEDA) and British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act give you a right to ask what I hold about you, to ask for a correction, and to challenge compliance. Email hello@oceanfallsinsurance.com. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia, or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

You can refuse analytics cookies in your browser. The contact form will not work if you send nothing, because I then have no way to reply.
