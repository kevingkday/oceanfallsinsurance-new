Integrated Risk Dynamics

Why AI Infrastructure Requires a Different Approach

Traditional commercial insurance places property, equipment, cyber, and business interruption into separate policies designed for conventional offices or light industrial spaces. High-density compute infrastructure does not fit neatly into these individual insurance categories.

warning

The Cascade Example

A power interruption can affect cooling, computing equipment and facility operations simultaneously, potentially creating equipment, business interruption and contractual exposures from a single event.

When high-density GPU racks drawing 40kW to 100kW+ per cabinet lose cooling circulation, thermal thresholds can be exceeded in seconds. Understanding how physical electrical switchgear, direct-to-chip liquid cooling manifolds, utility interconnects, and tenant SLAs intersect is essential to structuring effective coverage.

thermostat

High Thermal Densities

Accelerated thermal runaways require tight coordination between chiller breakdown, physical asset coverage, and telemetry-triggered response.

power

Power Dependency

Substation constraints, utility curtailment agreements, and transformer supply lead times create compounding operational downtime.

contract

SLA Commitments

Compute availability guarantees and tenant liquidated damages mean outages generate immediate contractual and balance sheet liabilities.

hub

Asset Concentration

Tens of millions of dollars in GPU clusters concentrated in compact data halls demand specialized aggregate sub-limits and valuations.

Comprehensive Analysis

The Interconnected Exposures of Compute Infrastructure

Modern AI data centres and GPU compute facilities require an interconnected risk strategy that evaluates every layer of the physical, electrical, digital, and environmental operating stack.

domain

Property

Physical building shell, data hall enclosures, structural slab load-bearing capacities, containment suites, and specialized gaseous fire suppression systems designed to protect sensitive electronics without water damage.

memory

GPUs, Servers & Compute

High-density accelerator clusters, NVIDIA H100/H200/B200 server chassis, high-speed optical transceivers, network fabric switches, and custom server racks evaluated on agreed-value replacement and market depreciation terms.

bolt

Electrical Infrastructure

Medium-to-low voltage step-down transformers, switchgear, busway power distribution, automatic transfer switches (ATS), uninterruptible power supply (UPS) batteries, and backup diesel/natural gas generation.

ac_unit

Cooling Systems

Direct-to-chip cold plates, coolant distribution units (CDUs), rear-door heat exchangers, closed-loop fluid piping, chillers, and cooling towers, covering mechanical breakdown, fluid leakage, and localized condensation hazards.

electric_meter

Utility & Power Dependency

Substation interconnections, utility transmission curtailment, power quality fluctuations, phase imbalances, voltage transients, and grid off-premises utility service interruption endorsements.

precision_manufacturing

Equipment Breakdown

Sudden and accidental electrical arcing, mechanical chiller failure, pump seizure, power harmonic distortion, transformer explosion, and rapid-onset overheating damages excluded by standard property forms.

trending_down

Business Interruption

High revenue-per-megawatt downtime loss, extended period of indemnity coverage, customer SLA penalty pass-throughs, and delayed revenue recovery while sourcing specialized replacement hardware.

construction

Construction & Commissioning

Course of construction/builders risk for data hall conversions, delayed startup (DSU) soft cost coverage, contractor wrap-up liability, and failures occurring during critical integrated system and load-bank testing.

security

Cyber Risk & Telemetry

Out-of-band management controller compromise (IPMI/BMC), industrial control system (SCADA/BMS) intrusion, unauthorized power/cooling manipulation, ransomware, and tenant network isolation breaches.

verified

Technology Errors & Omissions

Professional liability for managed compute providers, colocation hosting contractual disputes, failure to deliver promised power densities or uptime tiers, and system administration configuration oversights.

local_shipping

Supply Chain & Transit

Inland transit and ocean cargo for high-value GPUs and long lead-time equipment such as medium-voltage transformers, including shock/tilt sensor damage and replacement lead-time delay protection.

cyclone

Natural Catastrophe

Wildfire smoke particulate infiltration impacting air handlers and electrical contacts, extreme ambient heat waves derating cooling capacity, flood water exposure, and seismic structural disruption.

lock

Security & Political Violence

Physical perimeter intrusion, supply chain sabotage, civil commotion, and sovereign/political risk coverage where facilities operate across cross-border jurisdictions or sensitive data corridors.
