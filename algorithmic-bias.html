The AI / Modern Angle

Hiring tools, credit models and automated service decisions can draw discrimination claims against both the builder and the company that deploys them. I ask insurers how the form treats automated decisions, and what it excludes. I do not audit models, score fairness, or insure the outcome myself.

Coverage to ask about

Core Coverage Points

These are points I ask an insurer about. A policy includes one of them only if the wording says so.

groups

Employment Bias Defense

Protection against recruitment filter discrimination claims when AI hiring tools exhibit disparate impact on protected groups.

payments

Credit Scoring Liability

Defense for automated lending and financial decisions that may produce discriminatory outcomes across demographic groups.

gavel

Legal Defense Costs

Defence and settlement costs for a covered claim. Regulatory fines are often excluded or uninsurable.

build

Remediation Funding

Funding for mandatory bias audits, dataset rebalancing, and algorithmic retraining required by regulators or courts.

shield

Reputation Recovery

Crisis management and PR coverage for algorithmic bias incidents that threaten brand integrity and public trust.

Coverage questions, not an ethics audit

I can ask an insurer how a tech E&O or other liability form treats discrimination claims from automated decisions. Some forms exclude them. Some require you to disclose the tool.

A fairness review of a model is a different profession. I will not pretend a policy replaces it.
